"Commode chair" covers two products that solve different problems. One is a frame that sits over an existing toilet, so nothing has to be emptied. The other is a chair with its own bucket, for a room that has no toilet in it. In this range of six SKUs only two name over-toilet use in their specification; the rest are bucket chairs, and one of them publishes no load rating and no material at all.

Read the load column first. Four of six publish 150 kg. Two publish nothing — LM6301KG and LM502-S, and they are not the two cheapest or the two simplest by any obvious rule. In a category where the Italian, German and French tender documents all ask for a maximum user weight before they ask for anything else, a blank field is not a neutral answer.

Now read the two LM6108F rows together. Same platform, same material, same 150 kg, same waterproof leather seat, same five height levels. One carries a 30-unit MOQ, the other 500. Nothing in the load or material columns explains a difference of 16.7 times. It is explained by what the factory had to set up: the -2298 configuration runs as a standard build, while -157 is specified as a folding variant and publishes a 17 cm folded width that the other does not. Two variants of one frame, and a purchase order that says "LM6108F" identifies neither.

## What over-toilet use actually requires

Only LM6108F-2298 states over-toilet use among the commode SKUs. (The fourth toilet product, the elevated toilet chair frame in the [toilet safety range](/en/products/category/toilet-safety/), is a different article and sits on the toilet rather than over it.) Over-toilet use is a dimensional fit, not a feature flag, and three measurements decide it.

**Seat height.** A frame that sits over a toilet adds the height of its own frame to the height of the bowl. European WC pans typically present a rim between 40 and 45 cm. A frame that adds 5 cm puts the seat at 45–50 cm, which is a raised toilet seat — useful for a user who cannot sit low, wrong for one who can. This is why the five "height levels" on these commodes cannot be read as a range: five steps on one frame is a 5-level adjustment, and without a published span in centimetres the buyer does not know whether those five steps cover 10 cm or 25 cm. None of the six SKUs publishes the span.

**Bowl and hinge geometry.** An over-toilet frame straddles the pan and usually rests on the rim or on the floor beside it. Seat shape, the position of the lid hinges, and a close-coupled cistern all change whether the frame sits flat. A frame that rests on the rim on two points and hangs on the third is the most common fit failure in this product class, and it is not visible in a catalogue photo.

**The bucket is then redundant.** A frame bought for over-toilet use still ships with its bucket, and the distributor pays for a part the end user will not use. That is a line-item question with the factory, not a product-defect question.

## What the bucket decides

Only LM6301KG publishes its bucket: PP, oval, 33 × 28 × 13 cm, with two handles. That is 12.0 litres of nominal volume. The number matters because it sets the emptying interval, and the emptying interval sets who can live with the product.

A 12-litre bucket at a typical single void is good for several uses, which suits overnight and a bedside chair in a room without plumbing. It is not adequate where the user cannot be left alone for the interval it implies. Night-time use with a single carer, a care home with a fixed round, and a domestic arrangement where the user is alone during the day are three requirements one bucket size answers differently.

## Seat construction: leather, HD-PE, and blow-moulded

Three seat constructions appear in the six SKUs, and they behave differently under cleaning.

| Construction | SKUs | Cleaning | Failure mode |
|---|---|---|---|
| Waterproof leather over a board | LM6108F-2298, LM6108F-157, LM6301KG | Wipe, mild detergent; avoid prolonged soaking at seams | Seam separation, cracking at the fold line |
| Double-layer HD-PE, flip-up | LM6209KL | Washable, tolerant of stronger agents | Hinge and latch wear |
| Extra-wide blow-moulded HD-PE, double layer | LM6207L | Washable, no seams to fail | Frame, not seat, is the limit |

The leather seats look better in a showroom and generate the cleaning question. A care setting that uses a chlorine-releasing disinfectant on every surface will shorten the life of a seamed leather seat faster than an HD-PE one, because the seam is where the agent reaches the substrate. That is not a reason to avoid leather — it is lighter, warmer and quieter — but it is a reason to ask what the end customer cleans with.

Two of the six carry a patented flip-back leather backrest (LM6209KL, LM6301KG). A backrest that folds back is a transfer aid: it lets a carer move a user sideways without walking them round the chair, and it lets the chair sit closer to a wall. It is also an extra mechanism, and the hinge is what gets inspected after a year.

## Documents: the report that names two models out of six

The commode series holds an EU RoHS test report, number RC2503200997, issued 2025-04-01 by South China Technical Service, to RoHS Directive 2011/65/EU and (EU) 2015/863, tested on model LM6207 and stated to cover series LM6201–LM6209 and LM252.

Read that coverage list against the table above. LM6209KL and LM6207L fall inside it. LM6108F-2298, LM6108F-157, LM6301KG and LM502-S do not. This is not a defect in the documents — a series report covers the series it was written for — but it is exactly the check a distributor should run before answering a customer's "do you have CE?" with a PDF. A RoHS report is also not a CE certificate in the medical sense: it is hazardous-substance evidence, not a conformity assessment.

What the range holds at the system level is ISO 13485:2016, certificate 8623138319, issued by Huarenlian, valid to 2028-06-18, with an explicit scope covering commode chairs. That covers how the factory is run across all six models. Model-level conformity is a Declaration of Conformity per model under EU MDR 2017/745 Class I, and the numbers for those travel with the document package rather than being published — ask for the DoC that names the model on your order line, and check that the model on the paper matches the model in the carton.

## MOQ, price band and lead time

Commode chairs span the widest MOQ range in the catalogue: 30 units on LM6108F-2298, 100 on LM502-S, 300 on four SKUs, 500 on LM6108F-157. The published FOB band is US$10–15 with a 15–30 day window across all six. The 30-unit entry point is real, but a 30-unit order on one configuration will not reach a mixed container on its own — practically, the low-MOQ SKU tests a market and the 300-unit SKUs carry the container once the test works.

## Purchasing recommendation

1. **Decide over-toilet or standalone first.** Only LM6108F-2298 states over-toilet use. If the end user has a working toilet and no need for a bucket, the other five are the wrong product regardless of price.
2. **Never order "LM6108F".** The -2298 and -157 variants differ by a 16.7× MOQ and by folded width. The suffix is the product.
3. **Ask for the height span in centimetres.** Five levels is a step count, not a range. No SKU in the range publishes the span, and it decides whether the frame clears a standard WC rim.
4. **Match the RoHS report to the model before forwarding it.** RC2503200997 covers LM6201–LM6209 and LM252. Four SKUs in this range are outside it.
5. **Request the DoC by model, not by category.** ISO 13485 covers the factory; the Declaration of Conformity covers the product, and it has to name the model you are importing.

## FAQ

**Can any of these commodes be used over an existing toilet?**
LM6108F-2298 is the only SKU in the commode range that publishes over-toilet use. The elevated toilet chair frame in the toilet safety range is a different product and sits on the pan rather than over it.

**How much does the bucket hold?**
LM6301KG publishes a 33 × 28 × 13 cm oval PP bucket, 12.0 litres nominal. The other SKUs do not publish bucket dimensions.

**What is the load rating?**
Four of six publish 150 kg. LM6301KG and LM502-S publish no figure, so the rating has to be requested in writing for those two.
